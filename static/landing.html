Seven agents, one line. Each hands the next something it can check.

  1. Planner

    Reads your site. Hands on a customer, the pains they have, and where they talk.

  2. Scout

    Listens there. Hands on posts that describe a pain, each with its link.

  3. Qualifier

    Weighs each post. Hands on the few people worth writing to, and why.

  4. Enricher

    Looks for a way to reach them. Hands on a checked address, or says there is none.

  5. Writer

    Drafts from the person's own words. Hands on a message that quotes what they said.

  6. Reviewer

    A second model checks every claim against the evidence. Hands on a clean draft, or holds it.

  7. Sender

    Releases only what you approved, and only when every gate passes.

You give a goal, not labour.

The machine asks one question at a time, and only the ones it cannot settle itself. Every question shows the evidence beside it.

Autonomy is earned. The agents act alone only where their past judgments matched yours and their own checks are clean. One release you would have stopped ends autopilot.

What it will not do

  1. Pretend it can find your buyers when they do not write in public.

    It checks fit at intake and declines the product instead of padding a list.

  2. Email a lead. Not in this build.

    Approved mail goes to a demo inbox. The code has no path that addresses a lead.

  3. Send a claim the evidence does not show.

    The Reviewer holds the draft and says why.

Read what they wrote. Then decide whether to write back.
