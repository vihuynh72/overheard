The reason to write is already on the page

The usual way

Most outbound tools start from a list of companies and guess at relevance. The message has to invent a reason the reader should care.

This way

Earshot starts from a person’s own words about the problem. You write back to something they said, and you can show them where they said it.

How it works

Four steps, in the order they run. The last one is always yours.

  1. 01

    Describe the product

    Write one paragraph in plain words: what it does and who it is for. You do not bring a list of target companies.

  2. 02

    Earshot infers who it is for

    From that paragraph it infers a customer profile and five pain statements, written the way a frustrated engineer would put them in an issue.

  3. 03

    It searches issues by meaning

    It searches public issues for those pains by meaning rather than by keyword, and keeps the person who wrote each one, with a link to what they wrote.

  4. 04

    You read, then decide

    You read the evidence beside each draft, then approve, edit or reject it. Nothing leaves without your approval.

What it cannot do

It only hears people who write in public trackers. A run gives you dozens of leads, not thousands, and each one is worth reading.

Most people in a tracker list no company, so only some leads get a business email. When one is found it is checked with Hunter and labelled with the result.
